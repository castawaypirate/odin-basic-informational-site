Why I Started

I started this journey out of a passion for problem-solving and building things that live on the web. Instead of just consuming technology, I wanted to understand how it actually works under the hood and build software from scratch.

Why The Odin Project?

I chose The Odin Project because it emphasizes learning by doing rather than passively following video tutorials. It pushes me to read real documentation, think through edge cases, manage my own development environment, and build real-world projects with genuine autonomy

The Goal

My focus is on mastering full-stack JavaScript—from accessible, responsive user interfaces to resilient, performant backend architectures. My ultimate goal is to work as a full-stack web developer, contributing to impactful projects and continuously growing as an engineer.
